# Knowledge base: returns and refunds

## Eligibility

An item can be returned within **14 days** of delivery when it is:

- unworn and in its original packaging,
- accompanied by a receipt or an order number,
- not on the list of final sale items below.

Final sale items cannot be returned:

1. gift cards and store credit,
2. personalised or engraved products,
3. opened grooming and personal care items.

## How a refund is issued

Once a return is received and inspected, the refund is issued to the original
payment method. Card refunds take 5 to 10 business days to appear, depending on
the bank. Store credit is issued instantly and does not expire.

| Reason         | Return shipping | Restocking fee |
| -------------- | --------------- | -------------- |
| Damaged item   | free            | none           |
| Wrong item     | free            | none           |
| No longer want | customer pays   | 10 percent     |

> Note: a damaged or wrong item is always replaced or refunded at no cost to the
> customer. Ask for the order number and a short description, then file the report.

See also: `shipping-policy.md`, `warranty.md`.
